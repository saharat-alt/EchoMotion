## Status (as of 22 Nov 2026)
- Riemann hypothesis (Levent Alpöge + Claude, 20 Nov): Scientific American updated 21 Nov to "proves" / "later verified";
  OfficeChai reports a joint statement from the Lean groups ("We have full confidence ... is correct") — that X post could not be opened.
  AFP / Al Jazeera (earlier) still say unverified. Clay still lists it "Unsolved"; a prize needs publication + 2 years of acceptance.
- Navier-Stokes blow-up with no force (Alpöge + Tristan Buckmaster, 20 Nov, 79-page paper on GitHub, 600,000 lines of Lean):
  NOT verified; a computer-checked "certificate" file is not released yet. Tao: strategy "broadly clear", needs a proper written proof.
- Rival same day: Ravi Vakil (AMS president) released a separate "all human" Riemann proof by Alessandro Masullo.
- OpenAI, Sep 2026: ~10,000 AI agents, "88 hours", Navier-Stokes WITH a force — criticised as "an unrealistic, finely tuned force" / loophole.
- Credit dispute: Buckmaster alleged OpenAI tried to "scoop" his work; OpenAI denied.

## Facts with sources
- Clay: prizes announced Paris 24 May 2000, "$1 million allocated to the solution of each problem" https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/
- Riemann posed 1859 (Clay page) — press says "167 years"; Alpöge's note says "150 years". Thai InfoQuest: "กว่า 150 ปี".
- Alpöge note https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf : "I believe I have, working with Claude, discovered a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis."
  Written-out version "hit 250,000+ pages", so he released Lean only. Quote: "Claude flew, and danced, and sparkled near that finish line."
- Lean = "a program that checks every logical step" (The Next Web). Riemann proof ≈ 17 million lines of Lean (Kevin Buzzard via Al Jazeera).
- Navier-Stokes: "can blow up in finite time with no outside force acting on the fluid" https://thenextweb.com/news/navier-stokes-blow-up-without-force-buckmaster-alpoge ;
  authors "used Claude and Codex throughout" (Codex = OpenAI).
- Experts: Brian Conrey "I don't think any human was about to find this" (AFP https://phys.org/news/2026-11-anthropic-claude-math-greatest-riddle.html);
  Kevin Buzzard "we don't even know why the theorem is true" (Al Jazeera https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/riemann-hypothesis-has-the-worlds-most-famous-maths-problem-been-cracked);
  Andrew Sutherland (MIT) "a human triumph, and a day for celebration" (Sci Am https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/anthropic-mathematician-claims-to-prove-riemann-hypothesis-most-famous-problem-in-all-of-math/);
  Tao https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/11/20/unforced-euler-singularity-unforced-navier-stokes-singularity-and-the-generalized-riemann-hypothesis/
- Unverified: Maynard "98 or 99 percent certain" (second-hand). OfficeChai profile is low reliability. Unite.ai article is about September.
